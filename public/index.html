CHAPTER 02 // THE MICROCOSM

The 48 to 72 Hour Empirical Blindspot

Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) represents one of modern medicine's foremost challenges, associated with 4.95 million global deaths annually. In acute clinical practice, standard microbiological culturing and phenotypic AST require 48 to 72 hours.

Clinicians are forced to initiate empirical broad-spectrum antibiotic therapy without confirmation, leading to treatment mismatch rates exceeding 30% and accelerating selective resistance pressure.
